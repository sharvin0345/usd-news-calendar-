News trading rules

  • Do not open a new gold trade from 15 to 30 minutes before a high-impact USD number until 15 to 30 minutes after it.
  • Spreads widen when the number comes out. A stop that looked safe can get jumped.
  • Learn on a demo account, and use 0.01 lot.
  • This page is not financial advice. It is a clock and a reminder, not a signal to buy or sell gold.

Get alerts on iPhone

The calendar feed has high-impact USD events only. The phone alerts you 30 minutes before each one, and the time shows in Malaysia time when the phone is set to MYT.

Add to iPhone Calendar

Or add it by hand:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Calendar.
  3. Tap Accounts.
  4. Tap Add Account.
  5. Tap Other.
  6. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar.
  7. Paste the link below.
  8. Leave Calendar notifications on: Settings, then Notifications, then Calendar, then Allow Notifications.
https://sharvin0345.github.io/usd-news-calendar-/usd-news.ics

If the gold button opens a prompt, tap Subscribe. The feed refreshes on its own. Medium-impact events stay on this page and are not in the phone calendar. After you subscribe, keep Calendar notifications switched on or the 30-minute alert will not show.
